🎮 Controls & Adventure Guide

📱 Smartphone / Touch Controls

  • Bottom-Left Joystick: Drag with your left thumb in any direction to walk smoothly. Release to stop immediately.
  • Bottom-Right Action Button: Tap when near characters, glowing petals, signs, or portals to Talk, Collect, Examine, or Activate.
  • Direct World Tap: You can also tap directly on nearby highlighted characters or objects.

💻 Desktop / Laptop Controls

  • WASD or Arrow Keys: Move your traveler across the world.
  • E or Spacebar: Interact with nearby characters, items, and mechanisms.
  • I Key: Open your Starlight Satchel (Inventory).
  • J Key: Open your Quest Journal.
  • Esc Key: Pause game & audio settings.
