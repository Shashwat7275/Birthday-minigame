    🚀 How Personalization Storage & Sharing Works

    1. Local Browser Testing: Clicking Save All Changes stores your configuration in this browser's localStorage (completely separate from player save progress, so editing messages never erases gameplay progress).

    2. Publishing for Your Sister on Another Device: Because browser localStorage does not automatically sync across different phones, once you finish customizing everything, open tab G. Save, Export & Package and click "Download Bundled defaultConfig.js" (or click "Save Directly to Project Folder" if running via server.py) so your sister's phone loads your custom photos, names, and birthday letter immediately!
